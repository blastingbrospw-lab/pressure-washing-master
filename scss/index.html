House Washing / Soft Washing

We use low-pressure soft washing combined with biodegradable cleaning solutions to safely remove mold, mildew, algae, and built-up grime from your home's exterior. The result is a cleaner, brighter home — without the risk of damage that high-pressure washing can cause to siding and trim.

Concrete Cleaning

Years of Minnesota weather leave driveways, walkways, and patios looking stained and worn. Our surface cleaning equipment covers large areas quickly and evenly, removing deep-set dirt, oil stains, moss, and mildew — leaving your concrete looking like new.

Fence Cleaning

Whether you have wood, vinyl, or metal fencing, we tailor our cleaning approach to protect the material while thoroughly removing dirt, mold, and mildew. A clean fence can transform your yard's appearance and add years to the life of the fence.
