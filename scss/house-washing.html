House Washing & Soft Washing in the Twin Cities

Your home's exterior is constantly exposed to Minnesota's harsh elements — freezing winters, humid summers, and everything in between. Over time, mold, mildew, algae, dirt, and oxidation build up on your siding, leaving it looking dull, stained, and worn.

At Blasting Bros, we specialize in soft washing — a low-pressure cleaning method that uses biodegradable detergents to safely break down and rinse away biological growth and grime without damaging your siding, trim, caulk, or paint. Unlike traditional high-pressure washing, soft washing treats the root cause of staining rather than just blasting the surface.
