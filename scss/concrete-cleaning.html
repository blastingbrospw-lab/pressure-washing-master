Concrete Cleaning & Driveway Washing in the Twin Cities

Driveways, sidewalks, patios, and garage floors take a beating year after year. Oil drips, road salt, winter grime, tire marks, and biological growth like moss and mildew accumulate over time — and regular rain doesn't come close to washing it away.

Blasting Bros uses commercial-grade surface cleaning equipment that delivers consistent, even pressure across the entire surface — no streaks, no missed spots. We can tackle everything from a single driveway to large commercial parking areas, leaving concrete looking clean, bright, and fresh.

Why Choose Blasting Bros for Concrete Cleaning & Driveway Washing?

Surface Cleaner Equipment

We use rotary surface cleaners that clean evenly without leaving the stripe marks that a single wand creates.

Oil, rust, mildew, road salt residue, and tire marks are no match for our commercial pressure washing setup.
